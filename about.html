Our Commitment

The 5 Pillars of AH Lanka

Every transaction and journey is governed by five operational standards that protect our clients.

🛠️

Well-Maintained Vehicles

Every vehicle in our sales inventory and rental fleet undergoes rigorous multi-point mechanical inspections. From brake pads to air-conditioning compressors, we only operate vehicles in peak condition.

🛡️

Safe & Reliable

We provide full legal documentation for vehicle purchases and comprehensive passenger liability insurance for rentals. In the rare event of a mechanical issue, our rapid response team provides immediate backup.

🏷️

Competitive & Fair Rates

Transparent pricing without surprise add-ons. Our sales prices reflect true Sri Lankan market value, and our rental rates are straightforward and competitive for both short vacations and long-term hires.

🤝

Friendly Customer Service

Authentic Sri Lankan hospitality. We do not hide behind automated ticketing systems. When you message AH Lanka, you connect directly with experienced managers who listen to your needs.

🇱🇰

Islandwide Service & Coverage

Based in the North Western hub of Kurunegala, our network reaches Katunayake International Airport, Colombo, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Ella, and the southern coastline from Bentota to Mirissa.

💬

Zero-Friction WhatsApp Access

No mandatory account creation. No endless email chains. You tap a button, and you are instantly talking with AH Lanka’s official operations team on WhatsApp.
