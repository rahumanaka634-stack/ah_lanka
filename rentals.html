Driving & Rental Information for Sri Lanka

          
Everything foreign visitors need to know before hitting Sri Lankan roads.

        


        

          
          

            

              
              Driving Permits
            

            

              For Self-Drive, Sri Lankan law requires an endorsement of your International Driving Permit (IDP) from the Automobile Association of Ceylon (AAC). AH Lanka can assist you with this endorsement in advance!
            

          


          

            

              
              Airport Meet & Greet
            

            

              Landing at Katunayake Airport (CMB)? Our driver or representative will meet you at the arrivals hall holding a personalized name placard. We track your flight in real time for delays.
